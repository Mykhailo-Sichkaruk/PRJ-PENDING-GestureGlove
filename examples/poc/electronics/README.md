# Gesture glove — electrical prototype, revision 2

Open `glove/gesture-glove-v2.kicad_sch` for the circuit schematic, or
`fritzing/gesture-glove.fzz` in Fritzing's Breadboard view for the physical-looking
wiring diagram. The Fritzing parts and colored wires are separate editable
objects. Its background text is a separate annotation part.

## Read the drawings

- RF1–RF5: DIY resistive flex strips, drawn as variable resistors in KiCad.
- R1–R5: 10 kohm dividers to GND. C1–C5: 100 nF filters across those resistors.
- Each flex signal uses an ADC1-capable ESP32-S3 pin.
- U1: a finished Adafruit Feather ESP32-S3 #5323, with its charger/regulator.
  KiCad shows only the board connections used here. Pin numbers on this module
  symbol are logical interface names; it has no fabrication footprint.
- P1–P5 in Fritzing: one conductive fingertip pad each, with one electrical lead.
  SW1–SW5 in KiCad represent those pads touching the **same palm fabric**.
  They are not five purchased switches or permanently grounded fingertip pads.
- U2: GY-521 / MPU6050. VCC, GND, SCL, SDA and AD0 are connected; AD0 goes to
  GND for address 0x68. INT, XDA and XCL are unused and omitted in KiCad.
- SW6: a slide switch between EN and GND. Closing it disables the Feather
  regulator; it does not disconnect the battery or disable USB charging.
- BT1: a provisional 3.7 V, 500 mAh LiPo, wired to the board's battery socket.
  The external TP4056 listed in the original idea is superseded by the selected
  Feather's onboard charger in this PoC, not connected in parallel with it.

| Finger | Flex ADC / GPIO | Contact input / GPIO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thumb | A5 / 8 | A0 / 18 |
| Index | D5 / 5 | A1 / 17 |
| Middle | D6 / 6 | A2 / 16 |
| Ring | D9 / 9 | A3 / 15 |
| Little | D10 / 10 | A4 / 14 |

I2C: SDA = GPIO3, SCL = GPIO4. Enable internal input pull-ups for A0–A4;
contact with the grounded palm gives LOW. These are digital contact inputs,
not capacitive-touch inputs. Matching net labels in KiCad mean connected wires.
Crossing wires without a junction do not imply a connection.

## What is still provisional

This is a wiring proof of concept, not an electrically simulated or physically
tested device. DIY strip resistance and usable ADC range need measurement and
calibration. **GY-521 boards vary: confirm that the purchased variant works with
3.3 V at VCC before using this supply arrangement.** The exact battery lead
polarity and a suitable protected battery also need confirmation. Dimensions,
wire lengths, strain relief and mounting belong to the mechanical assembly.
The custom Fritzing part PCB views are illustrative; do not fabricate from them.

## Validation

`check_glove_connections.py` exports KiCad's netlist and compares its 16 nets
against Fritzing's native wire connections, including the Feather's internal
power/I2C buses. It checks that the five analog and five digital input nets,
power, I2C and EN connections agree. This catches pin-assignment errors that a
clean ERC alone would not catch. Fritzing's own application renders the supplied
breadboard SVG/PNG; KiCad exports the supplied schematic PDF.
